## What the defect was (verified in source) and what changed

`GuidedForceLayout.resolve(...)` sized the guided cover from a static, all-or-nothing
budget of hand-set per-section constants (`52 + 46 + bannerHeight + 44 + 84 + chartFloor
+ 52 + 6·gap + 24`), and gave the chart `floor + everything left`. When the `SET REST`
banner rendered taller than its reservation the estimate still returned
`essentialContentFits == true`, the chart absorbed the error, and the pause/skip row
landed past the screen edge — reachable only by scrolling (the `#938` `ScrollView` net).

## Per-AC verdicts (exact command, raw exit, observed line)

### AC1 — smallest supported device: everything visible without scrolling; Skip reachable without scrolling → **PARTIAL**

- *Skip/pause reachable without scrolling*: **PASS**. The row is pinned in its own bar
  below the `ScrollView`, so the chart height cannot move it. Observed on both
  simulator legs below (`controlsBar=64.0` … and in
  `docs/evidence/issue-993/se3-default-rest.png` the Skip button sits at the bottom
  edge while the content above it is scrolled to the top).
- *Every section visible without scrolling*: **FAIL by measurement on the smallest
  device** (and PARTIAL on the owner's device class). The measured numbers (below)
  show the SE covered frame is 647 pt and the fixed stack + trace floor + bar + gaps +
  padding measures **849.0 pt** — i.e. the stack overflows by 202 pt, and even with a
  **zero-height** trace it would still be 732.5 pt (85.5 pt over). Scrolling on the SE
  is therefore not removable by layout math; it needs ~86 pt less fixed content (or a
  shorter card set), which is a content/design decision outside this fence. On the
  owner's 402×778 device class the fixed stack + chrome is 730.4 pt, so 47.6 pt is
  left for the trace against its 140 pt floor: the trace is partially visible
  (`…/pro17-default-rest.png` shows the readout and the target band), and ~92 pt of
  the trace's bottom needs a small scroll. I did **not** shrink section constants to
  fake a fit (see "what I did NOT do").

### AC3 — fix the sizing decision (measured content and/or a pinned control bar), not the constants → **PASS (both legs)**

- **Measured fit**: the container reports the rendered padded stack, the trace's own
  height and the bar height through `onGeometryChange`; `GuidedForceLayout.resolve(...,
  measurement:)` substitutes the trace's floor for the trace and uses that measured
  essential height for the fit decision (the static budget remains only as the
  pre-measurement fallback, and it is now built from measured floors).
- **Pinned bottom control bar**: `controls(date:)` is rendered by the container under
  the `ScrollView` (`protocolContent`), not as the stack's last scrolling section, so
  the timer/End (top bar, first item) and Pause/Skip are co-visible at any chart
  height. `#899`'s shape is preserved: still no bottom `safeAreaInset`, no
  STOP/FINISH circle — the same row, moved out of the scrollable content.

### AC4 — cap the flexible chart → **PASS**

`GuidedForceLayout.chartMaximumHeight = 240`, and
`flexibleChartHeight = min(floor + slack, chartMaximumHeight)`; the new cap case pins
it (`testFlexibleChartStopsAtItsVisualMaximum`, plus the cap assertions inside the
measured-decision case).

### AC5 — reclaim the wasted top inset, state WHICH candidate + the measurement → **PASS**

Candidate **(a): double-applied safe-area padding** — measured, not assumed:

- The presented cover's content frame is *already* the safe-area rect:
  `viewport=375x647` on the SE's 375×667 screen, `viewport=402x778` on the iPhone 17
  Pro's 402×874 screen — while `geometry.safeAreaInsets` still reports the screen's
  insets (`insets.top=20.0` on the SE, `insets.top=62.0 insets.bottom=34.0` on the
  17 Pro).
- `.padding(.top, max(8, safeAreaInsets.top))` therefore added a **second** top inset:
  the top bar began 40 pt into the SE's 647 pt frame and 124 pt into the 17 Pro's
  778 pt frame (visible in
  `docs/evidence/issue-993/pro17-rest-before-inset-reclaim.png` / `se3-rest-before-inset-reclaim.png`);
  the same applied at the bottom (17 Pro: an extra 34 pt).
- Fix: the stack pads a fixed `8`/`12` pt. Effect measured with the same probe:
  17 Pro essential 968.4 → **870.4** (−76.0 pt: 54 top + 22 bottom reclaimed), SE
  essential 861.0 → **849.0** (−12 pt), and the dead gap above the first card is gone
  in `…/pro17-default-rest.png` / `…/se3-default-rest.png`. Before/after probe lines:
  `docs/evidence/issue-993/measurement-before-inset-reclaim.log.gz`.
