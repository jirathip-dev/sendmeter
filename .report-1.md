(`testRenamingATagToItsOwnNameSendsNothing`) stayed green.

outcome of `testOlderRenameAcknowledgementCannotOverwriteALaterRename` — it passed in
the RED leg. That test's convergence has two other layers: `confirmServerUpsert` refuses
a confirmation whose revision is not the row's current one (`LocalCacheStore:529-538`),

the chain itself — probe D.
