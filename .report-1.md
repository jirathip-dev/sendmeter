  constants — `derivedData 1 / filterOptions 1 / looseRecordings 3 / combinedItems 1` — and that walking every
  item/row afterwards adds **zero** scans.

### AC2 — invalidates on relevant session/recording/tag changes; typing, mode/type/tag selection and hidden-tag handling produce exactly the baseline identities and ordering — **PASS**

  hidden-tags-only, query+type+tag, hidden tag + query) assert **identical ids and order** for the All-mode timeline,
