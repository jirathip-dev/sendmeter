Sendmeter — Support

Help with the Sendmeter web app, iPhone app, and Apple Watch companion.

Contact

For app issues, feedback, or feature requests, email guyjrt10984@gmail.com.

Please include the device model, operating-system version, Sendmeter app version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not include passwords, health data, or other sensitive information.

Sign-in help

  • Use the email magic link or choose password sign-in on the login screen.
  • If a magic link has expired, request a new one and open only the newest email.
  • The Apple Watch receives its session from the signed-in iPhone app. Keep the paired devices nearby and open Sendmeter on the iPhone if the watch says it is waiting for the phone.

Force training

  • Live measured force requires a physical Tindeq Progressor and Bluetooth permission.
  • Without a sensor, choose an exercise and Force protocol, then tap Train without sensor to log timed external-load attempts.

Health and recovery

Recovery metrics appear after HealthKit permission is granted and Apple Health contains supported data. Sendmeter remains usable when permission is declined or no health data is available.

Account and privacy

You can delete your account and its associated data inside Sendmeter from Account → Delete account. For details about collected data and deletion, read the Sendmeter Privacy Policy.
