Send Log — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Send Log is a personal climbing training tracker. This policy covers the Send Log web app, iPhone app, and Apple Watch app.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (for sign-in only).
  • Training data you enter: climbing sessions (type, duration, perceived effort, notes) and training-phase history.
  • Force measurements: recordings from your Tindeq Progressor (force samples, peak/average values, tags and notes you add).
  • Workout data (watch): workout duration, heart rate summary, active energy, wrist elevation/motion-derived attempt data.
  • Health data (iPhone, with your permission): heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, sleep, and body weight, read from Apple Health on your iPhone — including data written there by any other apps or wearables you've connected — to compute a daily recovery score.

Where it lives

Your data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted on AWS in Singapore (ap-southeast-1), protected by row-level security: only your authenticated account can read or write your rows. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics SDKs.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties.
  • HealthKit data is used only to display your metrics and compute your recovery score. It is never used for marketing or advertising and is never disclosed to third parties, in line with Apple's HealthKit guidelines.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app: Account → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — every session, recording, workout, and health metric linked to your account is removed.
