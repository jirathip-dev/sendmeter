## Fix round: the layout-stability defect the measurement found

The first slice made the 17 Pro rest screen fit — and that exposed a feedback
loop in the #993 machinery: the chart's height was resolved from the measured
stack, and the re-measured stack depended on the chart's height. On the 17 Pro
rest screen the layout churned between two states (chart 151.7 ↔ 168.7,
`scrollContent` 697.0 ↔ 731.0, ~100 probe prints/second for the whole capture)
and the full-screen cover never drew (blank capture). Reproduced pre-fix in
`measurement-pro17-default-rest-prefix-churn.log` +
`-prefix-blank-capture.png`.

Fix (`2beab74`): resolve the growth's slack from the **static** budget, so the
chart no longer moves a quantity it depends on — its height is a pure function
of the viewport and the cycle cannot close. The fit gate and the essential
height still consume the measurement exactly as #993 shipped
(`measuredEssentialHeight` first, static budget only as fallback), controls
stay pinned, and growth still caps at `chartMaximumHeight`. On all five
measured legs the resolved chart equals its floor, and the 17 Pro rest leg is
stable and drawn. Residual risk disclosed: the measured stack itself can still
read slightly differently between renders on this device; nothing downstream
consumes that value except the fit check, and both readings on the borderline
leg agree with the drawn result.
