#991 fix round 2 — guard truth table, selection proof, static guarantee (2026-10-04 13:02 UTC)

Round 1 shipped `#if canImport(CoreFoundation)`; the ios-ci `package-tests` job (run 37203412812,
job 111439538188, at head 2acdf482) compiled that block ON LINUX — 80 `cannot find` errors at
ReadinessWidgetContract.swift:182-209 for CFString / CFPropertyList / CFPreferencesCopyValue /
CFPreferencesSetValue / CFPreferencesAppSynchronize / kCFPreferencesCurrentUser / kCFPreferencesAnyHost.
So `canImport(CoreFoundation)` is TRUE on Linux: swift-corelibs-foundation ships a CoreFoundation
SUBSET without those symbols. Round 2 replaces the module-availability guard with a target guard:

    #if os(macOS) || os(iOS) || os(watchOS) || os(tvOS) || os(visionOS)

(present 2x in ReadinessWidgetContract.swift: the accessor's compile guard and the
`appGroupStore` selection site; the health-core test gate uses the same condition.)
