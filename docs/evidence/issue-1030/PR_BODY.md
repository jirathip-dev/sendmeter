This is slice 1 of the #1028 rollout, ported from the approved prototype code rather than merged. #1031 and #1032 build on these tokens.

## What changed

- **One shared palette.** `SendLogWatchCore/SendmeterPalette.swift` is new and is the single source for phone and Watch.
  - `SendmeterStyle`, `ChartToken` / `ChartActivityHue` and `RecordingContextPalette` now resolve from it. The chart values are unchanged, only moved.
  - The Workout runner text colours, the Power/Strength identity dots, the Watch views and the Live Activity widget also resolve from it.
  - The Dashboard and Force tabs now sit on the palette canvas.
- **Palette gate.** `SendmeterPaletteGateTests` fails on any colour literal outside the 8 token files, on phone and Watch.
  - It includes a planted-literal self-test.
  - The white/black overlay count may only shrink. Its ceiling is 20, which is the count measured at this head.
- **Contrast.** `docs/design/tokens.md` is generated by `docs/evidence/issue-1030/tools/tokens.sh`.
  - It covers 81 text pairs, all AA in both themes, with 0 exceptions.
  - Its contrast table is byte-identical to the approved one.
  - Status pills use the hue mixed into text at 45 %, with a worst case of 4.71:1.
- **Chalk.** The texture appears on exactly two hero surfaces: Today's decision and the armed protocol.
  - It is fixed-seed and its opacity is capped by contrast (0.05).
  - It is hidden under Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency.
- **P6.** The Watch rest-target chips drop the per-duration mint/cyan/indigo/amber ramp. They now paint the phone's resting hue (`SendmeterPalette.rest` = `primary`), which is also the Watch's own resting band.
  - The cyan was pinned by the deleted Watch screenshot suite's selector-pixel assertion (`6203902`).
  - I replaced that pin on purpose with `testWatchRestTargetsPaintThePhoneRestingHue` and `testRestTargetHueIsThePhoneRestingHue`. The reason is in commit `1e6b013`.

I did not change any host setting. The identical command with a lane-local `-derivedDataPath` passes.
