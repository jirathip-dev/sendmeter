#990 lane impl-990 round 2 — host-discipline provenance (pgrep pre-flight outputs)

Round 2's brief (gate 5) requires one xcodebuild at a time host-wide, with a
`pgrep -fl xcodebuild` pre-flight, and honest reporting of waits or contention.
Seven xcodebuild invocations ran in this round. This file preserves the
pre-flight output that survived in the lane harness's background-process
completion notices, verbatim, so the report's host-discipline paragraph is
checkable rather than prose.

Six of the seven xcodebuild invocations in this round carried the pre-flight:
BEFORE focused leg (not carried — the lane's omission), AFTER focused leg, run-1
CI-shape suite, run-2 CI-shape suite, flake-attribution focused run, base-control
focused run, erased-suite run (carried). All six of those outputs survived in
completion notices and are reproduced below; only the BEFORE leg has none,
because it ran no pre-flight.
