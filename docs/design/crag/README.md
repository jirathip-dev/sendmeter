## Summary

Decision: Headlamp is gone. Every screen is now the dark iridescent moon look: deep space navy, moonlight white text, and the purple-teal-pink shimmer of the original loading screen on the buttons, the Send-o-meter and highlights. The scene is always the crag at night under the moon and stars; weather (dawn glow, rain, fog) shows inside it, with no day mode. The roo is the original black mascot with its iridescent edge and rounder ears. Nothing unlocks.
Screens: Today (4 weathers), Send conditions, Session, Board, route, Flashed!, Force, Log, Logbook, Settings, App icon, the full-size icon sheet, loading, the screenshot steps, board setup, wiring check, board import, old data, iCloud sync, empty, error, first run, the watch and the mascot sheet, at 390 and 1440 (59 images).
Open questions: none.

## What this round decides

- **Colour.** Deep space navy (#04061A to #232A5C) behind glass cards. Text is moonlight white (#EEF0FA) with a soft lavender-grey for secondary text. The one accent is the iridescent shimmer: purple #C2A8FF, teal #8FE6E0 and pink #FFB3DC, on **Start session**, Sent, the friction pill, toggles, the gauge, the force curve, chart bars and the week dots. Text on the shimmer is deep navy.
- **The scene is always night.** The crag, the tree, the pad and the chalk bucket sit under the moon, the stars and a faint violet haze. The weather comes from Open-Meteo, as in round 2, and shows inside the night scene:
  - Clear: full stars, and the roo watches the moon.
  - Dawn: a pink-violet glow on the horizon, and the roo chalks up.
  - Rain: streaks and a hazy moon, and the roo shelters under the boulder.
  - Fog: mist bands over the crag.
  - The cards stay the same dark glass in every weather; only the glass tint changes slightly. The boulder's top edge carries a thin iridescent rim of moonlight.
- **Today** shows the scene full-screen. Every other screen shows it blurred and dimmed behind its cards, as before. Settings › Scene has **Live weather** on, or you can pin Clear, Dawn, Rain or Fog. The day, golden hour and loading-screen choices are gone.
- **The Send-o-meter** is the moon: it fills with the shimmer from crescent (REST) to full (SEND), and the number sits on the lit side. The current zone's small moon gets a shimmer ring. HRV and Sleep are two small moons beside it, each filled to its share.
- **The roo** is the original mascot: a black body, a white muzzle, the die-cut iridescent edge, and shimmer on the cheeks and inner ears. Its ears are now rounder ovals. It has no headlamp. The same drawing is used in the scene, the moments, the empty and error states, the watch and the sticker icons. The Session tab silhouette has the same rounder ears.
- **Loading** is the original iridescent loading screen, polished. It keeps the original cave and moon art and the original roo mid-dyno, now with a soft shimmer wash over the cave, a halo and a few stars around the moon, a dotted dyno arc from a faint ghost of the roo below, an iridescent wordmark and an iridescent progress bar.
- **App icons:**
  - **Original**: today's icon, unchanged, and the default.
  - **Stickers**: eight poses of the original roo (dyno, crimp, chalk-up, flash, send, fall, rest and PR), all on one solid night background with the moon and a ledge, so the set reads on light and dark wallpapers.
  - There is no weather set and no unlocks.
- **Words.** Unchanged from round 2: plain **Start session** (VoiceOver reads the same), with slang only at the moments (**Flashed!**, **PR!**, "whipper", **Project**).
- **No scrolling during a session.** Session and Force each still fit on one screen on the phone (390 × 844) and on the watch.

## Render

`./render.sh` writes every image in `png/` using the headless Chromium in the host's Playwright cache (set `CHROME` to override). `SCREENS="today route" KEEP=1 ./render.sh` redraws only those screens. Open `index.html?s=<screen>&m=<weather>` to view one; the weathers are night, dawn, rain and fog.

Round 1 (four themes) is at commit `4b656d29`, and round 2 (Headlamp) is at `6f8a9a5a`.
